Naan Recipe

Delicious, with only 4 indredients!

Jump to: Ingredients | Tools | Instructions | Storing

These naans are great with curries, as wraps, or cut up with veggies and dip!

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups (180g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 cups (245g) greek yogurt
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Tools

  • Medium mixing bowl
  • Whisk or fork
  • Rubber spatula or wooden spoon
  • Bench scraper (or a knife)
  • Frying pan (Ideally cast iron or steel)
  • Metal cooling rack
  • Rubber tongs

Instructions

  1. Whisk 1 1/2 cups of all-purpose flour, 2 teaspoons of baking powder, and a 1/4 of teaspoon salt in a medium mixing bowl
  2. Mix in 1 cup of greek yogurt with a spatula or wooden spoon (I prefer a rubber spatula so I can use it to scoop the yogurt out of the measuing cup) until it is combined enough to knead with your hands
  3. Place the mixture onto the counter and knead until a dough forms
  4. Divide your dough into six pieces and let them sit for about ten minutes, or until puffy
  5. Roll your dough pieces into balls
  6. Lightly flour your surface and rolling pin, then roll the first ball in flour
  7. Begin to flatten it with the rolling pin, flipping and rotating it every few passes until thin (about half a centimeter deep)
  8. Repeat, stacking the naans in an inverse order so that the newest is always on the bottom (you want to cook the oldest first so each one gets time to sit before cooking)
  9. Next, place your pan on the stovetop and bring it to medium-high heat
  10. Once your pan is hot, place the first naan on its surface
  11. The naan should begin to bubble and fill with air, let it grow, gently lifting with tongs to peek at the bottom
  12. Cook until the naan is golden-brown to slightly blackened on the bottom bubbles, then flip and allow the other side to cook
  13. Place finished naans on a metal cooling rack and serve warm

Note:If you are comfortable multi tasking, you can turn your stove on while you begin to roll out your naans. Just roll them out while the pan is heating, you can usually get through 1-2 before it is hot. Then roll the others while cooking (I can typically roll out a naan in the time it takes to cook one)

Storing

I have found the best method for storing these naans is to wrap them in a dish cloth and close the wrapped naans into a large ziplock bag. You can usually store them in the fridge for up to 3 days (but mine never last that long).

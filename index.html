Basic Scone Recipe

The perfect base recipe for countless scone flavours.

Jump to: Ingredients Instructions Modifications

This is my favourite recipe for scones! I like to use greek yogurt and protein milk so I can pretend to be healthy while eating baked goods. I hope you enjoy your "healthy" scones!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (250g) all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup (113g) cold butter
  • 1/4 cup greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup add ins (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees Farenheit
  2. Combine 2 cups of flour, a 1/4 cup of sugar, 2 teaspoons of baking powder, and a 1/2 teaspoon of salt in a large bowl
  3. Cut in a 1/2 cup of butter
  4. Mix in a 1/4 cup of greek yogurt, a 1/4 cup of milk, and a 1/4 teaspoon of vanilla
  5. Form dough
  6. Fold in your 1/2 of add-ins if desired
  7. Form a round and cut into 8 wedges
  8. Sprinkle with sugar if desired
  9. Bake for about 15 minuted or until golden around the edges

Note: I recommend letting them cool for about 10 minutes before eating, as the centers may be a bit doughy when fresh out of the oven.

Some of my Favourite Modifications

  • Brown sugar and cinnamon
    • Replace granulated sugar with brown sugar (1:1 ratio)
    • Add a teaspoon (or more or less to taste) of cinnamon with the other dry ingredients
  • Lemon blueberry
    • Add the zest of one lemon to the dry ingredients
    • Add a 1/2 of bluberries as you add-in
    • Combine the juice of half a lemon with 1 cup of powdered sugar to make a glaze (let the scones cool before glazing)
